How it is built

Each note creates its own oscillator and gain node, so chords work. All notes share one filter and one delay.

Oscillator
An OscillatorNode makes the raw tone. Pitch follows 440 Hz × 2(note − 69) / 12.
Envelope
A GainNode shapes volume over time: attack, decay, sustain while the key is held, release after it is let go. Changes are scheduled, never jumped, to avoid clicks.
Filter
A low-pass BiquadFilterNode removes high frequencies. Lower cutoff sounds darker, higher resonance adds a peak at the cutoff.
Delay
A DelayNode feeds part of its output back into itself. Feedback stays below 100% so the echo fades out.
Output
A compressor stops loud chords from clipping. An AnalyserNode supplies the samples for the waveform display.
